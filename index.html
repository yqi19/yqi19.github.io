Yu Qi

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science, Northeastern University

I am a Ph.D. student at Northeastern University, advised by Prof. Lawson L. S. Wong. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Peking University in 2023.

I work on robotic manipulation, around one question: how can embodied systems generalize to objects, tasks, and environments they were never trained on?

Email| Google Scholar| GitHub

Research

  • How does data shape generalization? Scalable data collection and evaluation that expose the hidden biases limiting compositional generalization. (Scale Up Strategically, Two by Two)
  • How do pre-trained models help — and where do they fail? Diagnosing and enhancing the embodied and reasoning abilities of foundation and video models. (BEAR, MME-CoT, MME-CoF, MME-CoF-Pro)
  • How can embodied systems reason and act reliably? Structural inductive biases — equivariance, object-centric skills — for sample-efficient, generalizable control. (EquAct, Hierarchical Skill Learning)
