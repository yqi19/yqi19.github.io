Hi, I'm Yu Qi (祁煜), a Computer Science Ph.D. student at Northeastern University, working on scalable and generalizable policy learning for robot manipulation. In 2023 I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Peking University.

My research spans robot learning and computer vision, centered on three directions:

  • Scalable & generalizable manipulation. How robotic policies generalize compositionally, via bias-aware evaluation and data collection.
  • Multimodal reasoning & evaluation. Benchmarking chain-of-thought and reasoning coherence in large multimodal and video generative models.
  • Embodied capabilities of foundation models. Understanding and enhancing atomic embodied abilities of multimodal LLMs.
